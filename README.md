Shadows of Pasar Senja — a top-down stealth heist in three Indonesian nights. Slip through the night market, the heirloom museum and the dusk harbour, steal the Keris Pusaka, the Mahkota Senja and the Mutiara Pelabuhan, and reach the exit unseen. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's “Make the best stealth game” bounty, $50 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — or open `game/index.html` in desktop Chrome. No network, no build step.

## Play

WASD / arrows to move, SHIFT to crouch (slow, quiet, harder to see), CLICK to throw a coin and lure guards away, E to grab loot. Hide in tall grass, stay out of the lamplight, watch the vision cones: yellow means suspicious, red means the alarm is up. Cameras sweep the museum, a tripwire guards the hall, and a guard dog patrols the harbour. Finish a level without ever being spotted for the +$500 ghost bonus.
